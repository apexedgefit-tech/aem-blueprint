The AEM Blueprint is an educational tool, not medical advice. Every output — calories, macros, meals, timelines and training — is an estimate and a starting point, built on published equations (Mifflin-St Jeor and Katch-McArdle BMR, standard activity multipliers) and standard evidence-based training guidelines. Real-world results vary with adherence, sleep, stress and training; adjust with your own data. Consult a physician before changing your nutrition or training, especially with any existing condition.
